# PRODUCT — New Jersey Railing Works LLC

## Platform
- Platform: `web`
- Stack: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), photos as WebP files in `img/`, hosted on GitHub Pages
- Surface mode: Persuade (one-page site for a local trade)

## Users
- North Jersey homeowners planning or repairing railings, stairs, fences or gates. Most arrive on a phone, from a Facebook or Instagram link, often inside the in-app browser.
- The owner (Tony Tela) is the first audience: the site is given to him free and must look like his business done better than he expected.

## Purpose
- Turn a homeowner who saw the work into a call or text for an estimate.
- Show the work: the business's own photos are its strongest evidence.

## Positioning
- A hands-on New Jersey crew that does interior and exterior railings, full staircase renovations, fences and custom gates, start to finish (demo, install, stain and finish), in aluminum, iron, wood and vinyl.
- Source: their Instagram captions (@newjerseyrailings, seen 2026-10-19) and the Facebook group post (2026-10-19).

## Brand commitments
- Logo: round black badge, gold railing drawing with "NJRW" and "NEW JERSEY RAILING WORKS" (Instagram profile picture, 100px only).
- Colors: black and gold from the logo; page ground must not be black (house rule), so gold rides as trim on a dark non-black ground.
- Voice: warm, local, helpful, first-person plural ("We help homeowners…", "We'd be happy to help!").
- Their finishes: semi-gloss black powder-coated aluminum, red oak treads, iron balusters.
